# Vincent Mugondora Personal Portfolio - PRD

## One-liner

vincentmugondora.com is Vincent Mugondora's personal digital headquarters showcasing his software engineering work, AI projects, teaching, speaking, writing and entrepreneurial activities.

## For

The primary audience is:

- Technology companies
- Founders
- CTOs
- Engineering leaders
- Potential clients
- Event organizers
- Educational institutions
- Developers
- Students
- Potential collaborators
- Organizations interested in African technology

The secondary audience is anyone searching for Vincent Mugondora online.

## Success looks like

A visitor should understand within 5 seconds:

1. Who Vincent is.
2. What he builds.
3. What he teaches.
4. What problems he cares about.
5. How to contact or work with him.

The site should also establish Vincentmugondora.com as the authoritative online presence for Vincent Mugondora.

---

# Product goals

## Primary goals

1. Establish Vincent Mugondora as a credible technology professional.
2. Showcase high-quality software and AI projects.
3. Demonstrate engineering thinking rather than only visual work.
4. Establish Vincent as an educator and technical communicator.
5. Create a platform for speaking and collaboration opportunities.
6. Create a long-term home for Vincent's personal brand.
7. Build strong search visibility around Vincent Mugondora.

# V1 scope

The v1 website includes these six core areas:

### 1. Personal identity

The homepage clearly communicates:

- Vincent's identity
- Positioning
- Mission
- Current focus
- Professional CTA

### 2. Portfolio

Visitors can explore projects and detailed case studies.

### 3. AI Lab

Visitors can explore AI experiments and research/build notes.

### 4. Writing

Visitors can read technical and technology-related articles.

### 5. Teaching and Speaking

Visitors can understand Vincent's educational work, workshops, mentorship and speaking topics.

### 6. Contact

Visitors can contact Vincent for work, speaking, collaboration or other professional opportunities.

# Constraints

Tailwind CSS.

Static-first.

## Backend

None in v1.

## Content

## Hosting

Cloudflare or Vercel.

## Performance

Performance is a first-class requirement.

## Accessibility

WCAG-conscious implementation with keyboard navigation, semantic HTML and accessible contrast.

---

# Non-negotiables

- The site must feel premium and editorial.
- The site must not look like a generic developer template.
- The supplied visual reference is inspiration, not something to clone.
- Vincent's brand colors must remain consistent.
- Do not fabricate information.
- Do not invent achievements.
- Do not invent client names.
- Do not invent testimonials.
- Do not invent statistics.
- Mobile experience must be designed intentionally.
- SEO must be implemented from the beginning.
- Accessibility cannot be treated as a final-stage feature.
- Performance cannot be sacrificed for visual effects.
